X-23

X-23 is a female clone of Wolverine. Like Wolverine, X-23 has a regenerative healing factor, superhuman stamina, senses, speed, agility, and reflexes. She also has retractable adamantium-coated bone claws in her hands and feet. X-23 was created to be the perfect killing machine.

First Appearance:  NYX #3 (2004)
Creators:  Craig Kyle,  Christopher Yost
Powers:  Regenerative Healing Factor,  Bone Claws,  Superhuman Stamina, Agility, Reflexes, and Durability,  Superhumanly Acute Senses
