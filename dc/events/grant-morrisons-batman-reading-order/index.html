Grant Morrison’s Batman

Morrison began writing Batman in 2006 with issue #655,  introducing the character of Damian Wayne and beginning a seven-year-long run on the character across multiple titles.  This run is one of the most highly regarded Batman runs in history.

Year Published:  2006-2013
Featured Characters:  Batman,  Dick Grayson,  Damian Wayne
