Brightest Day

At the climax of Blackest Night a number of deceased heroes and villains were returned to life by the Entity. Now the resurrected search to understand the Entity’s reasons for bringing them back, and to deal with other consequences of their returns.

Year Published:  2010
Featured Characters:  Aquaman,  Hawkman,  Hawkgirl,  Martian Manhunter
