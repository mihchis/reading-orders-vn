The Question

The Question is Vic Sage, vigilante protector of Hub City. He is a great martial artist and detective, an investigative reporter in his civilian identity. When Sage was dying of lung cancer, he trained Renee Montoya as his successor and she eventually replaced him.

First Appearance:  Blue Beetle Vol. 5 #1 (1967)
Tác giả sáng tạo:  Steve Ditko
Powers:  Genius-Level Intellect,  Indomitable Will,  Expert Combatant,  Master Detective
