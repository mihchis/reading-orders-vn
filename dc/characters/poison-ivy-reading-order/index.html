Poison Ivy

Poison Ivy is a brilliant botanist turned eco-terrorist with the power to control plant life and produce deadly toxins. Once a researcher betrayed and experimented on, she gained immunity to poisons and the ability to manipulate flora on a massive scale.

Xuất hiện lần đầu:  Batman #181 (1966)
Tác giả sáng tạo:  Robert Kanigher,  Sheldon Moldoff
Powers:  Chlorokinesis,  Toxikinesis,  Toxic Immunity,  Genius-Level Intellect,  Seduction
