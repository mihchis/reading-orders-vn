Green Arrow

Green Arrow is a vigilante who fights crime using archery, technology, and martial arts. In his secret identity he is politician and billionaire Oliver Queen, a hot-headed social activist and owner of Queen Industries. The extended family of heroes who work with him call themselves Team Arrow.

First Appearance: More Fun Comics #73 (1941)
Creators:  Mort Weisinger,  George Papp
Powers:  Master Archer,  Expert Acrobat and Martial Artist
