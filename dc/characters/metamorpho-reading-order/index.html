Metamorpho

Metamorpho, real name Rex Mason, is a hero transformed by exposure to a mystical artifact known as the Orb of Ra. Once an adventurous archaeologist, Rex’s body was altered into a living chemical element, giving him the ability to transmute parts of himself into virtually any element or compound on the periodic table.

Xuất hiện lần đầu:  Brave and the Bold #57 (1965)
Tác giả sáng tạo:  Bob Haney,  Ramona Fradon
Powers:  Elemental Transmutation,  Metamorphosis,  Bio-Fission
