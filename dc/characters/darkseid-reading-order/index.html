Darkseid

Darkseid is the tyrannical ruler of Apokolips and one of the most feared beings in existence. A New God of immense power, he seeks the Anti-Life Equation to strip all free will from the universe and impose absolute order under his rule.

First Appearance:  Superman's Pal, Jimmy Olsen #134 (1970)
Creators:  Jack Kirby
Powers:  New God Physiology,  Omega Effect,  Genius Level Intellect,  Indomitable Will
