Blue Beetle and Booster Gold

When Ted’s uncle made an attempt to take over the world,  Ted set out to stop him,  recruiting the help of his archaeology teacher Dan Garrett – the first Blue Beetle.  In the course of the adventure,  Dan was fatally wounded and asked Ted to carry on the legacy of the Blue Beetle.

Booster Gold was born in the year 2442.  Traveling back in time and using the technology he stole from the 25th Century,  he became a superhero.  He uses his knowledge of historical events and futuristic technology to stage high-publicity heroics.

First Appearance:  Mystery Men Comics #1 (1939),  Booster Gold #1 (1986)
Creators:  Charles Nicholas Wojtkoski,  Dan Jurgens
Powers:  Genius-Level Intellect,  Power Suit,  Legion Flight Ring
