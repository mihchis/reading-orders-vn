Black Canary

Black Canary is a superhero vigilante who fights crime using martial arts and a sonic scream attack. She is one of the greatest fighters in the world, and also a strong tactical mind.

First Appearance:  Flash Comics #86 (1947)
Creators:  Robert Kanigher,  Carmine Infantino
Powers:  Canary Cry,  Master Martial Artist
