Huntress

Helena Bertinelli, once the daughter of a powerful mafia family, became Huntress after surviving their brutal murder, dedicating her life to dismantling organized crime. Trained in stealth, archery, and hand-to-hand combat, she’s a fierce operative who often walks the line between hero and avenger.

First Appearance:  Huntress #1 (1989)
Creators:  Joey Cavalieri,  Joe Stanton
Powers:  Indomitable Will,  Expert Martial Artist,  Peak Human Condition
