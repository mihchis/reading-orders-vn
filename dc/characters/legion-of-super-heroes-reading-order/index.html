Legion of Super-Heroes

The Legion of Super-Heroes is a 30th Century intergalactic team of super-powered young adults. Inspired by the legend of Superboy, they were founded by Cosmic Boy, Lightning Lad, and Saturn Girl.

First Appearance:  Adventure Comics #247 (1958)
Creators:  Otto Binder,  Al Plastino
Members:  Cosmic Boy,  Lightning Lad,  Saturn Girl,  Superboy,  Triplicate Girl,  Brainiac 5,  Phantom Girl,  Mon-El
