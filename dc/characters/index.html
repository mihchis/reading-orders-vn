Nhân Vật DC

Welcome to our list of DC Character Reading Orders.  The reading orders you will find here are different than your standard reading order.  Rather than contain a chronological list of every appearance a character has made these lists only contain issues that are important for that characters development or issues that are recommended reading.  All the important issues and legendary  writer runs are here.  Simply choose a character and begin reading.

The issue counters in the individual character reading orders do not include any events that may be in the reading order.
