Wonder Woman

Wonder Woman is an Amazon warrior princess. She was given power by the Gods to fight against evil in all its forms. Although she was raised entirely by women on the island of Themyscira, she was sent as an ambassador to the world of man, spreading their idealistic message of strength and love.

First Appearance:  All-Star Comics #8 (1941)
Creators:  William Moulton Marston,  Harry G. Peter
Powers:  Divine Empowerment,  Wisdom of Athena,  Master Combatant,  Indomitable Will
