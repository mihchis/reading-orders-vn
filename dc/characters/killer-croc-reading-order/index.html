Killer Croc

Killer Croc is a tragic and terrifying figure in Gotham’s underworld. Afflicted with a rare genetic condition that gives him reptilian skin, enhanced strength, and aquatic abilities. Whether in the sewers or among the Suicide Squad, Croc is a dangerous but sometimes misunderstood force of nature.

First Appearance:  Detective Comics #523 (1983)
Creators:  Gerry Conway,  Gene Colan,  Don Newton
Powers:  Regressive Atavism,  Regeneration,  Superhuman Strength
