Solomon Grundy

Solomon Grundy is an immortal zombie supervillain from the 19th Century. Originally murdered in the mysterious Slaughter Swamp, he resurrects and regenerates there every time he is killed again in his zombie form.

Xuất hiện lần đầu:  All-American Comics #61 (1944)
Tác giả sáng tạo:  Alfred Bester,  Paul Reinman
Powers:  Invulnerability,  Resurrection,  Superhuman Strength and Stamina
