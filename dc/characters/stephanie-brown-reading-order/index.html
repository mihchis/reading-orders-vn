Stephanie Brown

Stephanie Brown began her vigilante career under the guise of Spoiler. She was a frequent ally of Tim Drake. During his retirement, she briefly claimed his position and became Batman’s partner Robin. She later succeeded Cassandra Cain and became the newest Batgirl.

First Appearance:  Detective Comics #647 (1992)
Creators:  Chuck Dixon,  Tom Lyle
Powers:  Expert Acrobat, Hacker, Investigator, and Martial Artist
