Teen Titans

The Teen Titans were a team of young super-heroes who were the former sidekicks of older, more experienced heroes. They first came together when Robin, Aqualad and Kid Flash responded to an emergency. They fought a mysterious villain calling himself Mister Twister. After defeating Mister Twister the three young heroes decided to band together as the Teen Titans.

First Appearance:  Brave and the Bold #54 (1964)
Creators:  Bob Haney,  Bruno Premiani
Members:  Robin,  Kid Flash,  Aqualad,  Wonder Girl,  Speedy,  Beast Boy,  Raven,  Starfire,  Cyborg,  Superboy,  Red Robin
