Metal Men

Doc Magnus invented the Responsometer, a device that animates robots and gives them sentience. This allows the Metal Men to morph into liquid shapes but retain their solid form. Each member is named after the element they are made from, and their personalities reference properties of those elements.

Xuất hiện lần đầu:  Showcase #37 (1962)
Tác giả sáng tạo:  Robert Kanigher,  Ross Andru,  Mike Esposito
Thành viên:  Doc Magnus,  Gold,  Iron,  Lead,  Mercury,  Tin,  Platinum,  Copper,  Nameless
