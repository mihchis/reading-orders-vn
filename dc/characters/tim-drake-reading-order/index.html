Tim Drake

Tim Drake is a member of the Bat Family who has taken several names. Originally he became the third Robin when he figured out Bruce Wayne’s secret identity. Proving himself in combat, he was taken on for training, and would eventually become one of the world’s greatest detectives and martial artists. After Damian Wayne became the latest Robin, Drake assumed the identity of Red Robin.

First Appearance:  Batman #436 (1989)
Creators:  Marv Wolfman,  Pat Broderick
Powers:  Master Martial Artist, Acrobat, and Detective,  Genius-Level Intellect
