Doctor Fate

Kent Nelson is Doctor Fate, a powerful sorcerer and agent for the Lords of Order. His amulet, cloak and helmet are creations of the ancient Nabu, who acts as his mentor and spiritual guide.

First Appearance:  More Fun Comics #55 (1940)
Creators:  Gardner Fox,  Howard Sherman
Powers:  Magic,  Immortality,  Telekinesis
