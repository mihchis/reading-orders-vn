Jason Todd

A young street orphan who first encounters Batman while attempting to steal the tires off the Batmobile. Jason earns the Robin mantle a short while later by helping Batman apprehend the gang of thieves.

First Appearance:  Batman #357 (1983)
Creators:  Gerry Conway,  Don Newton
Powers:  Skilled Tactician and Detective,  Expert Martial Artist and Marksman
