Etrigan the Demon

Etrigan the Demon is a powerful, rhyming demon bound to the mortal Jason Blood by the wizard Merlin during the time of Camelot. Though often forced to fight for good, his demonic nature makes him unpredictable and ruthless, Jason struggles to control the beast within.

First Appearance:  The Demon #1 (1972)
Creators:  Jack Kirby
Powers:  Demonic Physiology,  Magic
