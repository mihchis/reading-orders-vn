Azrael

Azrael is a vigilante who formerly operated out of Gotham City. Most of his extraordinary abilities come from severe amounts of subconscious training and conditioning he was put through during his youth. Named after the Angel of Death, Jean-Paul Valley is actually descended from a long line of crimefighters using the Azrael suit and name.

Xuất hiện lần đầu:  Batman: Sword of Azrael #1 (1992)
Tác giả sáng tạo:  Dennis O’Neil,  Joe Quesada
Powers:  Enhanced Strength, Stamina, and Durability,  Enhanced Senses,  Expert Combatant
