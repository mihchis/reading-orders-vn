Power Girl

Power Girl is the Earth-Two equivalent of Supergirl, native Kryptonian and cousin to Superman. The distinctive costume she wears lacks an emblem, she uses this as an advantage to distract others in combat.

Xuất hiện lần đầu:  All-Star Comics #58 (1976)
Tác giả sáng tạo:  Gerry Conway,  Ric Estrada,  Wally Wood
Powers:  Solar Energy Absorption,  Heat Vision,  Enhanced Senses,  Flight,  Invulnerability,  Superhuman Strength, Stamina, Speed, Agility, and Reflexes,  Super-Breath
