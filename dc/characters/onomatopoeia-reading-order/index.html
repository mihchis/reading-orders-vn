Onomatopoeia

Onomatopoeia is a mysterious serial killer who hunts non-powered vigilantes with chilling precision. His identity remains unknown, making him one of the most elusive and terrifying killers to ever haunt Gotham City's heroes.

Xuất hiện lần đầu:  Green Arrow Vol. 3 #12 (2002)
Tác giả sáng tạo:  Kevin Smith,  Phil Hester
Powers:  Superhuman Durability
