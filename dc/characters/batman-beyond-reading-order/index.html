Batman Beyond

In 2039 Bruce Wayne is now a man in his 70s, living in isolation in Wayne Manor. Terry McGinnis is an athletic 16-year-old high school student and reformed juvenile delinquent with a deeply ingrained sense of personal justice. After stealing the Batsuit, Bruce initially opposes all of Terry's efforts and demands that he return the suit, but Terry convinces Bruce to let him take on the Batman mantle.

First Appearance:  Batman Beyond #1 (1999)
Creators:  Paul Dini,  Glen Murakami,  Alan Burnett
Powers:  Indomitable Will,  Stealth,  Hand-to-Hand Combat,  Acrobatics,  Tactical Analysis
