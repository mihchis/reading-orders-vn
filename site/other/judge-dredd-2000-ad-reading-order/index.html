Judge Dredd (2000 AD)

Judge Dredd is a law enforcement officer in the dystopian future city of Mega-City One in North America. He is a “street judge”,  empowered to summarily arrest,  convict, sentence,  and execute criminals.

Publisher:  Rebellion
Publication Date:  1977-2017+
Genre:  Science Fiction
Creators:  John Wagner,  Carlos Ezquerra,  Pat Mills

Reading Order

Judge Dredd featured in most progs (issues) of 2000 AD.  It may look like a lot but keep in mind each prog usually only has 6 pages of Judge Dredd story.  It is recommended to read the stories in a collected format.  The reading order is listed by collection,  further broken down into individual progs.

This reading order contains only the most highly recommended and/or major Judge Dredd stories.
