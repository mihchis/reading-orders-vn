Spider-Man

At a science exhibit Peter Parker was bit by a radioactive spider and gained incredible strength, agility, and the ability to cling to walls. Through his knack for science, he develops a gadget that lets him fire adhesive webbing of his own design through small, wrist-mounted barrels. Failing to act to stop an escaping thief, claiming that it was not his responsibility to do so, results in Peter’s Uncle Ben being killed. With great power there must also come great responsibility.

First Appearance:  Amazing Fantasy #15 (1962)
Creators:  Stan Lee,  Steve Ditko
Powers:  Wall-Crawling,  Superhuman Strength, Speed, Durability, Agility, and Reflexes,  Spider-Sense,  Indomitable Will,  Super-Genius Level Intellect,  Master Acrobat and Hand-to-Hand Combatant
