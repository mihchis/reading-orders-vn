Bane

Bane is a super-intelligent world-class fighter and tactical genius who enhances his great physical strength with a steroid called Venom, making him one of Batman’s greatest and most dangerous enemies. He is one of the only men ever to independently figure out Batman’s secret identity.

First Appearance:  Batman: Vengeance of Bane #1 (1993)
Creators:  Chuck Dixon,  Doug Moench,  Graham Nolan
Powers:  Superhuman Strength, Stamina, and Durability,  Enhanced Reflexes and Speed,  Genius-Level Intellect
