Cosmic Marvel (1966-2016)

The Cosmic side of Marvel is stranger and often more amazing then the adventures of the earth-bound heroes and villains. Infinity Gems. Warring alien species. World-devouring Galactus. Celestials. Cosmic Cubes. Elders of the Universe. Mad Titans. Abstract entities with unlimited power over reality and the basic concepts of the universe itself.

Year Published:  1966-2016
Featured Characters:  Thanos, Adam Warlock, Nova, Guardians of the Galaxy, Silver Surfer, X-Men, Galactus, Fantastic Four, Cosmic Entities
