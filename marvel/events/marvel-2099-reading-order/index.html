Marvel 2099

Marvel 2099 explores a possible, dystopian future of the Marvel Universe. North America is a corporate police state ruled by a few huge megacorporations, most notably Alchemax, which owns the private police force the Public Eye. There are no active superheroes in the world, and the previous heroes are mythologized through religion, as with the Church of Thor.

Year Published:  1992
Featured Characters:  Spider-Man, Doctor Doom, Punisher, X-Men
Previous Event:  Bloodties
Next Event:  Midnight Massacre
