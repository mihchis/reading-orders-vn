Blink

In the primary Earth-616 continuity, Blink was tense, panicky and frightened of her powers and could not control them.

The Age of Apocalypse continuity Blink showed a more refined power set and has mastered several methods of utilizing her powers.

First Appearance:  Uncanny X-Men #317 (1994),  X-Men: Alpha #1 (1995)
Creators:  Scott Lobdell,  Joe Madureira
Powers:  Bio-Molecular Spatial Displacement
