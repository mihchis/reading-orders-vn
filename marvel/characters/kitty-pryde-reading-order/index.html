Kitty Pryde

Kitty Pryde is the youngest person invited to join the team of mutant superheroes the X-Men. During her early years she acted as a “kid sister” to many older members of the X-Men. Pryde possesses a “phasing” ability that allows her and objects or people with which she is in contact to become intangible. This power also disrupts any electrical field she passes through, and lets her simulate levitation.

First Appearance:  Uncanny X-Men #129 (1980)
Creators:  Chris Claremont,  John Byrne
Powers:  Phasing,  Expert Martial Artist,  Genius-Level Computer Expert
