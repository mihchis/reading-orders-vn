X-Force

An offshoot of the X-Men, X-Force takes a more militant and aggressive approach towards its enemies compared to the X-Men. X-Force was involved in attacks on mutant groups such as the Mutant Liberation Front and was itself branded early in its career as a group of terrorists.

First Appearance:  New Mutants #100 (1991)
Creators:  Fabian Nicieza,  Rob Liefeld
Members:  Cable,  Cannonball,  Boomer,  Domino,  Feral,  Shatterstar,  Siryn,  Warpath,  Wolverine,  X-23,  Caliban,  Archangel,  Deadpool,  Fantomex
