Man-Thing

Former scientist Ted Sallis was transformed into a creature composed of vegetable matter through the synergistic interaction of mystical energy and swamp mutagens. It is able to sense human emotions, and is enraged by fear and automatically secretes a poisonous chemical; anyone feeling fear and clutched by the Man-Thing is prone to be burned.

First Appearance:  Savage Tales #1 (1971)
Creators:  Stan Lee,  Roy Thomas,  Gerry Conway,  Gray Morrow
Powers:  Immortality,  Empathy,  Acidic Secretion
