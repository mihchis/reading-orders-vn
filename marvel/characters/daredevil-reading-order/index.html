Daredevil

As a child Matt Murdock was blinded by a radioactive substance that fell from an oncoming vehicle. While he could no longer see, the radioactive exposure heightened his remaining senses beyond normal human ability and gave him a type of sonar which acts as his vision.

First Appearance:  Daredevil #1 (1964)
Creators:  Stan Lee,  Bill Everett
Powers:  Superhuman Sensory System,  Radar Sense,  Skilled Attorney,  Expert Detective,  Tracker,  and Martial Artist
