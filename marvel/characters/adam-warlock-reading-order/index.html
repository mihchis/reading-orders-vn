Adam Warlock

Adam Warlock is a powerful artificial being created to be the perfect human by a group of Earth scientists known as the Enclave. Though noble, he struggles with his darker counterpart, Magus, representing the corruption of his potential. Adam Warlock stands as both savior and threat to the cosmos.

Xuất hiện lần đầu:  Marvel Premiere #1 (1971)
Tác giả sáng tạo:  Stan Lee,  Jack Kirby
Powers:  Superhuman Physiology,  Immortality,  Energy Manipulation
