Spider-Woman

At a young age Jessica Drew became gravely ill from months of uranium exposure. To save her life, her father injected her with an experimental serum based on irradiated spiders’ blood.

First Appearance:  Marvel Spotlight #32 (1977)
Creators:  Archie Goodwin,  Jim Mooney
Powers:  Superhuman Strength, Speed, Stamina, Durability, Agility, Reflexes, and Hearing,  Wall Crawling,  Venom Blasts
