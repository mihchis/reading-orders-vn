Gambit

Gambit has the ability to mentally create, control, and manipulate pure kinetic energy. Gambit is also incredibly skilled in card-throwing, hand-to-hand combat, and the use of a staff. Gambit is known to charge playing cards and other objects with kinetic energy, using them as explosive projectiles.

First Appearance:  Uncanny X-Men #266 (1990)
Creators:  Chris Claremont,  Jim Lee
Powers:  Molecular Acceleration,  Expert Marksman and Combatant,  Master Thief
