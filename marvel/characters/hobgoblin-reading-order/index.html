Hobgoblin

The Hobgoblin is a calculating menace who stole the Green Goblin’s secrets and improved them. Armed with enhanced strength, explosives, and a glider, he favors manipulation over madness, engineering chaos from the shadows while others take the fall.

Xuất hiện lần đầu:  Amazing Spider-Man #238 (1982)
Tác giả sáng tạo:  Roger Stern,  Mike Zeck,  John Romita Jr.
Powers:  Superhuman Strength, Speed, Agility, Reflexes, Stamina, and Durability,  Regenerative Healing Factor
