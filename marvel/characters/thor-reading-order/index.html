Thor

Thor is the blood-son of Odin, All-Father of the Asgardians, and Jord, who was also known as Gaea, the goddess who was one of the Elder Gods. Odin sought to father a son whose power would derive from both Asgard and Midgard.

First Appearance:  Journey Into Mystery #83 (1962)
Creators:  Stan Lee,  Larry Lieber,  Jack Kirby
Powers:  Godlike Strength, Invulnerability,  Superhuman Speed, Stamina, and Senses,  Longevity,  Flight,  Indomitable Will
