High Evolutionary

The High Evolutionary is a brilliant and morally ambiguous geneticist obsessed with advancing evolution. He developed technology to accelerate genetic mutation, creating entire species such as the New Men, animal-human hybrids. Operating from his space-faring base, Counter-Earth, the High Evolutionary often acts as both a godlike creator and a dangerous manipulator.

Xuất hiện lần đầu:  Thor #134 (1966)
Tác giả sáng tạo:  Stan Lee,  Jack Kirby
Powers:  Superhuman Strength, Intelligence, and Durability,  Telepathy,  Master Geneticist
