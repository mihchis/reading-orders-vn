Domino

Domino often uses her skills as a mercenary, but has also been the partner, confidante, and lover of Cable, a lieutenant in his militant group X-Force, and a member of the X-Men.

First Appearance:  New Mutants #98 (1991)
Creators:  Fabian Nicieza,  Rob Liefeld
Powers:  Probability Manipulation,  Enhanced Agility & Reflexes,  Expert Marksman
