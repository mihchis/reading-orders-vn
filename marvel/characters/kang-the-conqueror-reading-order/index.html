Kang the Conqueror

Kang the Conqueror is a time-traveling warlord and one of the most dangerous threats to the multiverse. Born Nathaniel Richards in the 31st century, he discovered time travel and used his advanced technology to conquer entire civilizations across history. A master strategist with an army spanning centuries, Kang sees himself as the rightful ruler of time.

First Appearance:  Avengers #8
Creators:  Stan Lee,  Jack Kirby
Powers:  Superior Intellect,  Brilliant Tactician,  Indomitable Will
