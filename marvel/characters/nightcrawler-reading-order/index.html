Nightcrawler

Nightcrawler is the son of Mystique and the demon warlord Azazel. His physical mutations include indigo-colored skin, two-toed feet and three-fingered hands, yellow eyes, pointed ears and a prehensile tail.

First Appearance:  Giant-Size X-Men #1 (1975)
Creators:  Len Wein,  Dave Cockrum
Powers:  Teleportation,  Superhuman Agility and Reflexes,  Master Acrobat and Combatant
