Cable

Nathan Summers was born to Cyclops and Madelyne Pryor, a clone of Jean Grey. As a child he was infected with the Techno-Organic Virus and taken to the future in the hopes of finding a cure. Eventually he returned as a grown man, Cable, and existed at the same time as his infant self.

First Appearance:  Uncanny X-Men #201 (1986)
Creators:  Chris Claremont,  Rick Leonardi,  Louise Simonson,  Rob Liefeld
Powers:  Omega-Level Telekinesis,  Telepathy
