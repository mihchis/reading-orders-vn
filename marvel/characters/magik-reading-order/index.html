Magik

Magik is the younger sister of the mutant Colossus. Her natural mutant power is the ability to teleport. She also is a capable sorceress.

First Appearance:  Giant-Size X-Men #1 (1975)
Creators:  Chris Claremont,  Dave Cockrum
Powers:  Sorcery,  Stepping Discs
