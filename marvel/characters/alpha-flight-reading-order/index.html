Alpha Flight

Alpha Flight is Canada’s premier superhuman defense team, operating under federal mandate to protect national and global security. Core members include Guardian, a technologically enhanced leader; Sasquatch, a gamma-powered powerhouse; Shaman and Snowbird, wielders of Indigenous and Arctic mysticism; and Northstar and Aurora, mutant speedsters and light manipulators.

First Appearance:  Uncanny X-Men #120 (1979)
Creators:  Chris Claremont,  John Byrne
Members:  Guardian,  Sasquatch,  Shaman,  Snowbird,  Northstar,  Aurora,  Puck
