Deadpool

The ‘Merc with a Mouth’ is another product of the Weapon X program. His accelerated healing factor allows him to survive even decapitation and incineration. Deadpool’s insanity allows him to break the 4th wall.

First Appearance:  New Mutants #98 (1991)
Creators:  Fabian Nicieza,  Rob Liefeld
Powers:  Healing Factor,  Weapons Master
