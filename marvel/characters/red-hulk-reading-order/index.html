Red Hulk

Red Hulk is a mysterious gamma-powered powerhouse who suddenly appeared as both rival and counterpart to the Hulk. Unlike the original, he retains his tactical mind in battle, wielding military precision along with immense strength.

Xuất hiện lần đầu:  Hulk Vol. 2 #1 (2008)
Tác giả sáng tạo:  Jeph Loeb,  Ed McGuinness
Powers:  Superhuman Strength, Stamina, and Durability,  Regenerative Healing Factor,  Energy Absorption
