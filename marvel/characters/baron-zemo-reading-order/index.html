Baron Zemo

Helmut Zemo is the 13th Baron Zemo. His father taught him the idea that the Master Race should rule the world. Helmut would ultimately follow in his father’s footsteps as a supervillain using his family’s money and his own scientific know-how to recreate his father’s work.

First Appearance:  Captain America #168 (1973)
Creators:  Tony Isabella,  Sal Buscema
Powers:  Gifted Intellect,  Expert Marksman,  Expert Tactician
