Nick Fury

Nick Fury was the second commander of S.H.I.E.L.D. Under Fury, S.H.I.E.L.D. grows into one of the world’s most powerful organizations, reaching covertly into national governments and forming strategic alliances with the Avengers and other superhero groups, while always maintaining independence and deniability.

First Appearance:  Sgt. Fury and his Howling Commandos #1 (1963)
Creators:  Stan Lee,  Jack Kirby
Powers:  Infinity Formula,  Master Tactician and Strategist,  Advanced Military Operator,  Expert Marksman
