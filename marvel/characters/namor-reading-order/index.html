Namor

Namor is the mutant son of a human sea captain and a princess of the mythical undersea kingdom of Atlantis. He became the Prince of Atlantis, and a warrior for his people against the “surface-dwellers.”

First Appearance:  Motion Picture Funnies Weekly #1 (1939)
Creators:  Bill Everett
Powers:  Flight,  Superhuman Strength, Durability, Stamina, Speed, and Agility,  Underwater Breathing,  Aquatic Telepathy
