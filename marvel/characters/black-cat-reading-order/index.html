Black Cat

Felicia Hardy is the daughter of a world-renowned cat burglar. After suffering a traumatic event as a college freshman, she trained herself in various fighting styles and acrobatics and adopted her costumed identity as the Black Cat.

First Appearance:  Amazing Spider-Man #194 (1979)
Creators:  Marv Wolfman, Keith Pollard
Powers:  Probability Field Manipulation,  Olympic-level Athlete,  Expert Thief,  Skilled Martial Artist
