Arcade

Arcade affects a manner of dress and speech that makes him appear to be a comedic character. His preferred method of murder is an underground funhouse of colorful deathtraps, which he has dubbed “Murderworld.”

First Appearance:  Marvel Team-Up #65 (1978)
Creators:  Chris Claremont, John Byrne
Powers:  Genius-level Intellect
