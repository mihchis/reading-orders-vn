Marvel NOW! Reading Order Part 1

Part One of the Marvel NOW! reading order contains dozens of new series and major shifts in the status quo. The five original X-Men are back. The teams of X-Force, the Defenders, and the Thunderbolts consist of new members. There is a new, superior, Spider-Man swinging through the streets of New York. The Avengers and New Avengers series see the beginnings of an epic story that will culminate years later with the Secret Wars (2015) event.
