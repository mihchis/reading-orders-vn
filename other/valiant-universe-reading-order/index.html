Trang chủ Other Valiant Universe Reading Order

Valiant Universe

In 2012 Valiant re-launched their line of comics. Starting with X-O Manowar, the other launch titles were Harbinger, Bloodshot, and Archer & Armstrong. Shadowman later joined the line up to complete the re-launch of the new Valiant Universe. Over the next several years Valiant would continue to launch new series, creating an excellent alternative to the shared universes of Marvel and DC.

Publisher:  Valiant Entertainment
Publication Date:  2012-Present
Genre:  Superhero
Creator:  Jim Shooter,  Bob Layton
