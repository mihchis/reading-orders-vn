Spawn

Al Simmons was a highly trained assassin, murdered by his employer and former friend. Ending up in Hell he sold his soul to Malebolgia, one of the many rulers of the Hell, in order to see his wife Wanda again. Malebolgia had other things in mind for Al and sent him back to earth as a Hellspawn, one of his soldiers, to do his bidding.

Publisher:  Image Comics
Publication Date:  1992-Present
Genre:  Superhero
Creator:  Todd McFarlane
